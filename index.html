Google Developer Groups on Campus

GDG on Campus SATI Vidisha

Official platform of Google Developer Groups on Campus at Samrat Ashok Technological Institute (SATI), Vidisha, Madhya Pradesh, India.

About Our Chapter & Institutional Legacy

Established in 1960, Samrat Ashok Technological Institute (SATI) is an autonomous engineering college situated in Vidisha, Madhya Pradesh. For over six decades, SATI has championed academic excellence, scientific innovation, and technical leadership in central India.

Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGoC) SATI Vidisha extends this heritage by fostering an active, student-led developer community. Operating under the global Google Developer Groups program, we provide a collaborative incubator where students bridge the gap between classroom theory and industry practice through peer-to-peer learning, hands-on workshops, hackathons, and open-source contributions.

Core Technology Tracks & Curriculum

Our chapter offers structured learning pathways and hands-on code labs across multiple technical domains:

  • Modern Web Development: Full-stack web architectures utilizing React 19, Vite, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, RESTful & GraphQL APIs, responsive design, and Core Web Vitals optimization.
  • Android & Mobile Engineering: Native application development using Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material Design 3, Coroutines, StateFlow, Room database, and publishing on Google Play.
  • Cloud Computing & DevOps: Infrastructure and backend engineering on Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Firebase, Docker containerization, Kubernetes orchestration, and continuous integration pipelines.
  • Generative AI & Machine Learning: Practical AI integration utilizing the Google Gemini API, prompt engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), TensorFlow, PyTorch, and multimodal AI agents.
  • Open Source & Google Solution Challenge: Collaborative software engineering addressing the 17 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) through student-built solutions.
  • Competitive Programming & Data Structures: Algorithmic problem solving, data structures masterclasses, and software engineering technical interview preparation.

Chapter Programs & Community Initiatives

  • Technical Workshops & Study Jams: Interactive, guided code labs on modern web frameworks, mobile SDKs, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Annual DevFest Conference & Hackathons: Regional developer gatherings featuring technical keynotes, coding marathons, and innovative solution showcases.
  • Peer Mentorship & Portfolio Reviews: Senior student mentors and alumni guiding junior developers with resume optimization, project architecture, and code reviews.
  • Solution Challenge Sprints: Multi-week design and development sprints tackling real-world social and environmental challenges.
